        Settings

        Weekly goals

        How many you aim to publish each week (Monday to Sunday). The Dashboard shows your progress and your streak of weeks that met every goal. Leave empty for no goal.

        Reminders

        Checked once an hour while Chrome is running, at most one notification an hour. Only things scanned in the last 14 days count.

        Impression levels

        Each item is compared with your usual (median) impressions for that type on that platform: very high (green) is 2× or more, high (blue) 1.25×, medium (yellow) around your usual, low (orange) under 0.75×, very low (red) under 0.4×. The number of filled bars shows the same level. Levels appear once you have 5 items of a type.

        Your data

        Backup and restore

        A backup holds your items, scans, follower counts and settings. Load it into another browser or a newer version.

        Accounts

        Stats of each LinkedIn or X account stay apart. The Dashboard shows the account you are signed in to.

        Help

        Everything is stored only in this browser. Nothing is sent to any server.
